Adventures in Fire Island

Fire Island National Seashore consists of 26 miles of the 32-mile long barrier island called Fire Island. The seashore encompasses 19,579 acres of marine and terrestrial property within its boundaries, including Smith Point County Park, located at the eastern end of the island, and 17 communities that pre-dated the Seashore's establishment. Several bay islands, sand flats, and wetlands north of the of the barrier island lie within the seashore's boundary. Approximately 15,000 acres are submerged in the Great South Bay or Atlantic Ocean.
